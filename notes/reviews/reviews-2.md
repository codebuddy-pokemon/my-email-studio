# Reviews 2: RHEL 9 Hands-On Admin Workshop

Made-up practice reviews of Petafyve's earlier RHEL 9 Hands-On Admin Workshop. Not real customers.

---

**21. Laura P., Linux admin. ★★★★★**
Best RHEL training I've done. The instructors run these systems for clients every week and it shows in every answer.

**22. Ahmed R., sysadmin. ★★★☆☆**
The Ansible lab guide had two steps in the wrong order. We ran the playbook before the inventory was set up and it failed for everyone.

**23. Zoe F., DevOps engineer. ★★★★★**
Rootless Podman with systemd quadlets was exactly what our team needed. I've already moved two services over.

**24. Carlos M., platform engineer. ★★★★☆**
Very hands-on. I'd have liked more than a morning on Ansible, it's the part I use most.

**25. Emily S., junior admin. ★★★★★**
The SELinux lab where you fix a denied service using audit logs was a lightbulb moment. Clear and practical.

**26. Viktor N., sysadmin. ★★☆☆☆**
Waited almost a week after booking to get the pre-course setup email. I nearly cancelled because I didn't know if my place was confirmed.

**27. Jade L., Linux admin. ★★★★☆**
Storage module with LVM and Stratis was well taught. The lab VM ran out of disk halfway through, which was annoying.

**28. Arjun D., infrastructure engineer. ★★★★★**
Instructors went off-script to help with a real kernel panic one of us had at work. Very generous with their time.

**29. Isabel C., DevOps engineer. ★★★★☆**
Good course. The firewalld lab instructions used an old zone name, it took a while to figure out what they meant.

**30. Felix B., cloud engineer. ★★★★★**
Image Builder and the golden image lab saved me weeks of trial and error. Exactly the kind of thing you only learn hands-on.

**31. Amara O., sysadmin. ★★★☆☆**
Liked the content. The materials and lab guide only reached me on the second day, so day one was hard to follow.

**32. Lucas H., Linux admin. ★★★★★**
Boot troubleshooting was the highlight. Breaking and fixing GRUB and the initramfs, then doing it again without notes.

**33. Rosa T., platform engineer. ★★★★☆**
SELinux was fantastic. Only gripe: the module felt short, I'd trade some of the Cockpit time for more SELinux.

**34. George K., sysadmin. ★★★★★**
Practical, no-nonsense instructors. Every question got a straight answer with an example.

**35. Yuki A., DevOps engineer. ★★★☆☆**
The Podman lab instructions said to run as root in one step and rootless in the next without saying why. Confusing.

**36. Patrick E., Linux admin. ★★★★☆**
Good systemd module. I wanted more on timers and journald, we rushed through them at the end of day two.

**37. Diana W., junior admin. ★★★★★**
I was nervous about the level but the labs built up step by step. I left confident on SELinux and Ansible basics.

**38. Omar J., infrastructure engineer. ★★☆☆☆**
My lab environment crashed during the final challenge and couldn't be restored in time. Disappointing end to a good course.

**39. Freya M., sysadmin. ★★★★★**
The kernel tuning lab with tuned and sysctl was practical and immediately useful for our database hosts.

**40. Hugo S., cloud engineer. ★★★★☆**
Strong course overall. Lab access came late on day one, but the instructors gave us extra time to catch up.
