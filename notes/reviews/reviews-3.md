# Reviews 3: RHEL 9 Hands-On Admin Workshop

Made-up practice reviews of Petafyve's earlier RHEL 9 Hands-On Admin Workshop. Not real customers.

---

**41. Anna K., Linux admin. ★★★★★**
The Ansible module was superb. Writing idempotent roles against real RHEL 9 hosts beat any tutorial I've followed.

**42. Mohammed Y., sysadmin. ★★★☆☆**
Lab guide for the storage module was hard to follow. It mixed LVM and Stratis steps on the same page and I mixed them up.

**43. Sara L., DevOps engineer. ★★★★★**
Podman was the reason I signed up and it delivered. Building, running and auto-updating containers with systemd, all hands-on.

**44. James T., platform engineer. ★★★★☆**
Instructors were excellent. I'd have liked a full day on SELinux instead of half a day.

**45. Olivia R., junior admin. ★★★★★**
Every lab was on a real VM I could break. That's how I learn. The boot recovery lab was my favourite.

**46. Chen W., infrastructure engineer. ★★☆☆☆**
Lab login details came by email at 8:55 for a 9:00 start, and went to my spam folder. Rough first morning.

**47. Maya G., sysadmin. ★★★★☆**
Liked the firewalld lab a lot. My VM's network dropped twice, which slowed the exercise.

**48. Noah P., Linux admin. ★★★★★**
Instructors clearly build RHEL platforms for a living. They shared real war stories and the fixes that worked.

**49. Leila H., DevOps engineer. ★★★☆☆**
The Ansible lab instructions didn't say which user to run the playbook as. Several of us hit permission errors.

**50. Erik S., cloud engineer. ★★★★★**
Image Builder lab was practical and well paced. I left with a working blueprint for our base image.

**51. Tara N., sysadmin. ★★★★☆**
Great SELinux content. Wanted more time on custom policy modules, we only touched on them.

**52. Joseph B., Linux admin. ★★★★★**
Kernel tuning was a highlight. I never knew tuned profiles could make such a clear difference on our hosts.

**53. Ines F., platform engineer. ★★★☆☆**
Course workbook arrived as a download link four days after the course. Would have been useful during the labs.

**54. Ryan C., junior admin. ★★★★★**
Hands-on throughout, friendly instructors, and labs that felt like real tickets. Highly recommended for newer admins.

**55. Nadia M., sysadmin. ★★★★☆**
systemd module was good but too short. Timers, targets and drop-ins in one hour was a lot.

**56. Pedro A., infrastructure engineer. ★★☆☆☆**
The lab environment was unstable on day three. My VM became unresponsive during the Podman lab and had to be reset.

**57. Kate J., DevOps engineer. ★★★★★**
The Podman and quadlet lab was the clearest explanation of rootless containers I've seen.

**58. Samir D., Linux admin. ★★★★☆**
Boot troubleshooting was excellent. The lab instructions for the rescue target step were a bit vague, but the instructor helped.

**59. Lily O., sysadmin. ★★★★★**
SELinux used to scare me. After this course I read audit logs and fix denials without guessing.

**60. Max E., cloud engineer. ★★★★☆**
Very good course. Ansible was the strongest part. I'd come back for an advanced Ansible follow-up.
