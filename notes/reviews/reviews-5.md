# Reviews 5: RHEL 9 Hands-On Admin Workshop

Made-up practice reviews of Petafyve's earlier RHEL 9 Hands-On Admin Workshop. Not real customers.

---

**81. Sofia B., Linux admin. ★★★★★**
SELinux, finally explained by people who use it every day. The audit log troubleshooting lab was excellent.

**82. Harry M., sysadmin. ★★★☆☆**
Booked three weeks ahead and still only got lab access details the night before. Stressful.

**83. Nora K., DevOps engineer. ★★★★★**
The Ansible labs were realistic and well built. I wrote a patching playbook I now run every month.

**84. Ravi S., platform engineer. ★★★★☆**
Podman was great. I wanted another hour on it, especially on building images with Buildah.

**85. Ella T., junior admin. ★★★★★**
Instructors made a hard topic feel manageable. Boot troubleshooting was my favourite lab.

**86. Dominic F., infrastructure engineer. ★★★☆☆**
The firewalld lab instructions said to reload but not which command. Small thing but it tripped up half the class.

**87. Ayesha R., sysadmin. ★★★★★**
Every module was hands-on on real RHEL 9 VMs. Kernel tuning and tuned profiles were a nice extra.

**88. Jack L., Linux admin. ★★★★☆**
Liked the Image Builder lab a lot. It ran short, I'd have liked to build an image for a second cloud target.

**89. Mira D., DevOps engineer. ★★☆☆☆**

**90. Theo G., cloud engineer. ★★★★★**

**93. Lucy P., platform engineer. ★★★★★**
