# Reviews 1: RHEL 9 Hands-On Admin Workshop

Made-up practice reviews of Petafyve's earlier RHEL 9 Hands-On Admin Workshop. Not real customers.

---

**1. Priya N., Linux admin. ★★★★★**
The SELinux module finally made contexts click for me. Relabelling a broken web root in the lab was exactly the problem I had at work last month.

**2. Tom R., sysadmin. ★★★★☆**
Great instructors who clearly run RHEL in production. My lab login only arrived the morning of day one, so I started the first lab ten minutes late.

**3. Aisha K., DevOps engineer. ★★★★★**
The Ansible playbooks section was the best part. We wrote a role to harden a fresh RHEL 9 host and I used it at work the following week.

**4. Marcus L., Linux admin. ★★★☆☆**
Good content, but the Podman lab steps skipped how to log in to the registry. I lost twenty minutes before an instructor pointed it out.

**5. Elena V., platform engineer. ★★★★★**
Hands-on from start to finish. No slide marathons. The boot troubleshooting lab, where they break GRUB on purpose, was brilliant.

**6. Sam O., junior admin. ★★★★☆**
I learned a lot about systemd units and timers. I wish we had spent more than one hour on them, it felt rushed.

**7. Hannah B., sysadmin. ★★☆☆☆**
My lab VM froze twice during the storage module and I had to wait for a rebuild. Lost most of the LVM exercise.

**8. Raj P., infrastructure engineer. ★★★★★**
The instructors answered real questions from our own environment, not just the course script. Worth it for that alone.

**9. Chloe D., Linux admin. ★★★★☆**
Loved the firewalld and nftables lab. The instructions for the rich rules exercise were a bit unclear, though, and half the room got the zone wrong.

**10. Ben W., DevOps engineer. ★★★★★**
Podman and rootless containers were explained better here than anywhere I've read. Clear, practical, no fluff.

**11. Fatima S., sysadmin. ★★★☆☆**
Course materials PDF came three days after the course ended. I wanted it during the labs, not after.

**12. Leo M., cloud engineer. ★★★★★**
Image Builder was new to me and the lab showed a real use: building a golden image for our cloud. Very practical.

**13. Grace H., Linux admin. ★★★★☆**
SELinux module was excellent. I wanted more time on it, we only got through half the troubleshooting scenarios.

**14. Daniel F., sysadmin. ★★★★★**
Instructors know RHEL 9 inside out. The kernel tuning lab with tuned profiles was a nice surprise.

**15. Mei T., platform engineer. ★★★☆☆**
Lab instructions for the Stratis exercise referenced commands that didn't match the RHEL 9 version on the VM. Confusing for a while.

**16. Oscar J., junior admin. ★★★★★**
As someone newer to RHEL, I liked that every module ended with a real break-and-fix challenge. Learned more in three days than in months alone.

**17. Nina C., DevOps engineer. ★★★★☆**
Ansible section was great. My lab cluster wasn't ready when we started, I waited about half an hour for access on day two.

**18. Kwame A., Linux admin. ★★★★★**
The Cockpit demo was short but useful. Mostly I loved that we did everything on real VMs, not a simulator.

**19. Sophie G., sysadmin. ★★★★☆**
Good balance of theory and labs. The boot troubleshooting lab was my favourite, I'd happily do a whole day of it.

**20. Ivan K., infrastructure engineer. ★★☆☆☆**
The lab environment kept dropping my SSH session in the afternoon. Content was fine but I spent too long reconnecting.
