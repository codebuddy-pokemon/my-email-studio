# Petafyve brand voice

How Petafyve sounds in every email. Petafyve is a cloud solutions provider and Red Hat delivery partner that builds enterprise cloud infrastructure on RHEL, OpenShift and OpenStack. Our readers are DevOps engineers, OpenShift engineers and Linux admins.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Practical, direct, peer-to-peer.**

We write like a senior engineer helping a colleague: someone who has done the work, respects the reader's time and gets to the point.

- Keep emails short and skimmable: under 150 words, with one clear call to action.
- Use technical terms without explaining them. Our readers know what a pod, a node or a subscription is.
- No humour, no jokes, no puns. Stay straight to the point.
- Say "you" and "your", not "customers" or "users".

## 2. Words we use

- hands-on
- production-ready
- your cluster
- Plain, exact technical names: RHEL 9, OpenShift 4.x, OpenStack, Ansible

## 3. Words we avoid

- revolutionary
- game-changing
- seamless
- synergy

If a sentence needs one of these words to sound exciting, rewrite it with a concrete fact instead.

## 4. Claims we must never make

1. **No uptime promises.** Never say "100% uptime", "zero downtime" or anything like them.
2. **No exam guarantees.** Never promise that someone will pass a certification exam. Say the training *prepares* them.
3. **No speaking for Red Hat.** Never suggest that Petafyve is Red Hat or speaks for Red Hat. We are a Red Hat delivery partner, and we say exactly that.
4. **No exact savings figures.** Never promise cost savings like "cut your cloud bill by 50%". Describe how we help, not a guaranteed number.

## 5. How we sign off

Every email ends with:

```
The Petafyve engineering team
petafyve.com
```

Then the footer from `.claude/rules/emails.md`, which says why the reader is getting the email and has our address and an unsubscribe link.
